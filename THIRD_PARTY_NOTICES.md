# Attribution and Reuse

This repository reorganizes experimental records and source snapshots. It does not claim authorship of the inherited circuit techniques or replace their original attributions. Cite the manuscript for scientific contributions and this repository's exact release for the preserved evidence.

The circuit snapshots derive from [Layr-Labs/ecdsafail-challenge](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/ecdsafail-challenge) and the experiment branches of [jieyilong/ecdsafail-challenge](https://github.com/jieyilong/ecdsafail-challenge). Their original source revisions are recorded in [the freeze](provenance/freeze.json) and [source index](sources/README.md).

The upstream [NOTICE](sources/UPSTREAM_NOTICE.txt) is restored alongside the source archives. It identifies `src/circuit.rs`, `src/sim.rs`, and `src/weierstrass_elliptic_curve.rs` as reused from the ZKP ECC project under CC BY 4.0. That notice includes the authors, upstream repository, DOI, and license link. It applies to the stated files, not automatically to every file in this repository.

Original copyright notices and source text are retained. This publication does not assign a new blanket license or relicense material whose permissions are not specified by its source. Public availability is not a substitute for checking the applicable upstream terms.

Repository curation, wrappers, and documentation were prepared with AI assistance under Jieyi Long's direction. Scientific attribution remains with the paper's authors and the original circuit contributors. The new organization changes paths and presentation, not frozen observations or analysis.
